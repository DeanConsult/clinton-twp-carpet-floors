Our Story
20+ Years of Flooring Excellence

Clinton Township Carpet and Floors is the best place to call for any of your flooring needs. We have over 20 years of experience in all aspects of flooring and have an amazing reputation in the Clinton Township community.

We carry a huge selection of carpeting with different types of styles, colors, materials, and price ranges. We also have a great selection of other flooring such as hardwood floors, laminate floors, ceramic tile, custom vinyl tile, and many more.

At Clinton Township Carpet and Floors we bring our store to your door and bring tons of samples for you to match up with your wall colors and furniture. There is no longer a need to drive around town to all the retail carpet stores and big box stores. Please give us a call the next time you have a new flooring project.
